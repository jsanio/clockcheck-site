ClockCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

ClockCheck is an iPhone app that measures how accurately a mechanical watch keeps time by listening to it tick. It is operated by Tidemark Advisory LLC (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy: hello@clockcheck.app.

The short version

  • The app does not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The microphone is used only while a check is running. The audio is analysed on your iPhone. It is never saved and never leaves your phone.
  • Your Watch Box (your watches, photos and saved checks) is stored only on your iPhone. We never receive it.
  • ClockCheck is free and has no purchases. If you choose to leave a tip on Buy Me a Coffee’s website, that is a separate service (see “Tips”).

Microphone

ClockCheck asks for microphone access so it can hear your watch tick. The microphone is on only while a check is running, and stops when the check ends or you leave the app. The sound is turned into measurements on your iPhone and then discarded. No audio is recorded to a file or sent anywhere.

Your results

A check is not saved unless you choose to save it to a watch in your Watch Box (see below). Until then, the latest result is kept in memory so it can be shown on screen, and it is gone when the app closes. If you tap Share Result, ClockCheck makes an image of the result and hands it to the iPhone share sheet. Where it goes after that (Messages, Photos and so on) is up to you.

The Watch Box

The Watch Box lets you keep a collection. This is what it holds, and only if you add it:

  • Watches: brand, model, reference, movement, type, beat rate, dates (bought, serviced), notes, and whether it is in your collection or on your wish list.
  • Photos: a photo of a watch, if you take one with the camera or choose one from your photo library. ClockCheck keeps its own copy inside the app. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo.
  • Saved checks: when you choose to save a check to a watch, ClockCheck keeps the numbers (rate, beat error, beat rate, confidence, time and date, watch position) and a small graph of the check. Audio is never saved. A check is saved only when you choose to save it.

All of this is stored only on your iPhone, in a small database and a photo folder inside the app. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Because it is part of the app’s data, it is included in your iPhone’s normal backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, if you use them). Those backups are held by Apple or on your computer under your own settings, not by us.

Tips: “Buy me a coffee”

ClockCheck is free. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. If you would like to say thanks, clockcheck.app has a “Buy me a coffee” link, and so does the Result screen for customers of the US App Store. A tip unlocks nothing in the app.

The link opens buymeacoffee.com in your browser. That website and the payment are run by Buy Me a Coffee, not by us, and its own privacy policy and terms apply. The app sends nothing to it. If you leave a tip, Buy Me a Coffee shares with us what it gives page owners: the amount and date, and the name, email address and message you enter. Buy Me a Coffee may also show your name and message on its public page for ClockCheck; its own policy explains your options. We use what we receive only to say thanks and to keep our business records, and we do not sell it or use it for marketing.

What else is stored or used on your iPhone

  • A note that you have seen the introduction, so it isn’t shown every time. It contains nothing about you or your checks.
  • Which country’s App Store you use. ClockCheck reads this on your iPhone only to decide whether to show the “Buy me a coffee” link on the Result screen. It is not saved and not sent to anyone.

Ratings

ClockCheck may ask whether you would like to rate it. Ratings are handled by Apple’s App Store, and ClockCheck does not see who rated or what they chose.

How to delete your data

  • One watch: open the watch and delete it. This also deletes its saved checks and its photo from the app.
  • Everything: delete the ClockCheck app. This removes the Watch Box and its photos from your iPhone.
  • If you use iCloud Backup, older backups may still hold a copy until you delete them (Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Backups). Photos you took or chose from your own photo library are not touched when you delete a watch or the app.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you left a tip and want us to delete the record we received, write to hello@clockcheck.app.

Apple

ClockCheck is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy.

Children

ClockCheck does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
